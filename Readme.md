# VC Men's 2025 Program

## 2025 Theme: "Forging Disciples"

### **Introduction**
The VC Men's 2025 Program is designed to mentor men through faith, fellowship, and leadership, focusing on building strong, faith-centered individuals who can become servant leaders in their communities. Inspired by the Netflix film *The Forge*, the program emphasizes transformation, perseverance, and leadership.

---

## **Annual Theme: "Forging Strong Men"**
The theme symbolizes the shaping and refining of character, relationships, and leadership through intentional activities. Each session aligns with this overarching theme, drawing lessons from *The Forge*.

---

## **12-Session Curriculum**

### **Winter: Breakfast Sessions**
- **January: "The Spark of the Forge"**
  - **Activity:** Small group session with leaders before January ends.
  - **Focus:** Share reflections on *The Forge* and its lessons for personal transformation.
  - **Leadership Action:** Discuss specific scenes and their relevance to the VC Men’s program.

- **February: "Forging Purpose"**
  - **Activity:** Breakfast and Introduction to Ministries.
  - **Focus:** Reflect on purpose and ministry involvement.
  - **Leadership Action:** Pair new members with mentors.

- **March: "Forging Endurance"**
  - **Activity:** Breakfast.
  - **Focus:** Explore themes of perseverance.
  - **Leadership Action:** Launch a "Perseverance Challenge" for the next three months.

---

### **Spring: Outdoor Activities**
- **April: "Forging Bonds"**
  - **Activity:** Backyard Choma (Barbecue).
  - **Focus:** Build deeper connections through fellowship.
  - **Leadership Action:** Team-building activities inspired by the movie.

- **May: "Forging Integrity"**
  - **Activity:** Golf Outing & Mother’s Day Luncheon.
  - **Focus:** Highlight integrity in leadership.
  - **Leadership Action:** Share examples of integrity during the luncheon.

---

### **Summer: Outdoor and Open Sessions**
- **June: "Forging Action"**
  - **Activity:** Community Service Day.
  - **Focus:** Align with themes of giving back and leadership.
  - **Leadership Action:** Assign leaders to coordinate the project.

- **July: "Forging Sacrifice"**
  - **Activity:** Wilderness hike.
  - **Focus:** Reflect on personal sacrifices.
  - **Leadership Action:** Plan a reflection session post-hike.

- **August: "Forging Unity"**
  - **Activity:** Men’s Retreat.
  - **Focus:** Foster unity and collaboration through discussions.
  - **Leadership Action:** Leadership workshop with role-playing inspired by *The Forge*.

---

### **Fall: Indoor Activities**
- **September: "Forging Growth"**
  - **Activity:** Backyard Choma.
  - **Focus:** Discuss growth through struggles.
  - **Leadership Action:** Create personal and spiritual growth plans.

- **October: "Forging Wisdom"**
  - **Activity:** Men’s Seminar.
  - **Focus:** Leadership lessons inspired by *The Forge*.
  - **Leadership Action:** Assign members to share reflections.

- **November: "Forging Gratitude"**
  - **Activity:** Backyard Choma.
  - **Focus:** Highlight gratitude as a leadership quality.
  - **Leadership Action:** Organize a Thanksgiving drive.

---

### **Winter: Celebratory Session**
- **December: "Forging Legacy"**
  - **Activity:** Christmas Dinner.
  - **Focus:** Celebrate achievements and honor emerging leaders.
  - **Leadership Action:** Present the vision for the upcoming year.

---

## **Additional Activities and Leadership Development**
- **Mentorship Programs:** Pair members for guided mentorship.
- **Workshops:** Host monthly leadership training sessions.
- **Community Engagement:** Incorporate service projects.
- **Recognition:** Honor leaders during events like the Christmas Dinner.

---

## **Get Involved**
This program offers opportunities for spiritual growth, community building, and leadership development. Join us as we forge a stronger community of disciples in 2025!
